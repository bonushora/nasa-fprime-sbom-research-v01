This is a documentary redistribution review, not legal advice.

## Original research materials

The R4 record identifies Thales Rangel, Independent Researcher, as author and rights holder for the original research scripts, minimal harness, and EN/PT-BR manuscripts, with authorization to license those materials under Apache-2.0. Candidate R6 preserves that representation; it does not invent a signature, institutional approval, or rights in third-party material.

Apache-2.0 applies only to the authorial files identified in [LICENSE-SCOPE.md](LICENSE-SCOPE.md). It does not relicense F Prime, Syft materials, the SPDX schema, generated SPDX metadata, names, trademarks, or external content.

## Third-party and generated material

- F Prime license and notice are preserved verbatim under `third-party/fprime/`.
- The Syft checksum asset is accompanied by the Syft project's Apache-2.0 license and a source notice under `third-party/syft/`.
- The SPDX 2.3 schema is accompanied by a source, hash, attribution, official copyright notice, and CC-BY-3.0 license URI under `third-party/spdx-spec/`.
- Each generated SPDX declares `dataLicense: CC0-1.0`. Embedded package metadata and references are preserved as generated and are not presented as authored or exclusively owned by the research author.
- `CITATION.cff` intentionally omits a package-wide `license` field because this candidate is a mixed-rights collection.
